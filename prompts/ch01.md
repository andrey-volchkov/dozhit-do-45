### pro_01_lamp — 16:9 — обязательный
*Кабинет следователя, 1938. Круг настольной лампы, остальное тьма.*
> Night interrogation office, 1938. Top-down three-quarter view of a desk covered with worn green baize. A single metal desk lamp with a conical shade throws a hard circle of light onto the baize; everything outside the circle falls into total black. A man's hand in a dark sleeve with a plain cuff slides a small cabinet photograph face-down into the circle of light with two fingers. Beside it: a glass of cold tea in a metal holder, a blotter, a sharpened pencil. Thin cigarette smoke hangs in the light. Hard noir chiaroscuro, deep shadows, cold silver tones, heavy 35mm grain. Oppressive silence, stillness before a question.

### ch01_01_group — 4:5 — обязательный, главный кадр игры (вариант А)
*Кабинетная карточка утром 18 октября 1905 года, если Митя не пустил Сеньку. Порядок слева направо строгий: игра смазывает Митю, подкрашивает бант и приглушает фигуры маской.*
> Formal studio group portrait of four children, a Kiev photo atelier, morning of 18 October 1905, full-length figures. They stand on a worn carpet in front of a painted canvas backdrop of a classical stone balustrade with a painted vase of flowers; the canvas shows creases and a patched corner. Strictly left to right, evenly spaced, each figure clearly separated by a small gap: (1) a thin 12-year-old Jewish boy with dark curly hair, a quick clever grin, collar askew; (2) a serious 12-year-old boy in a patched gymnasium jacket, arms at his sides, holding his breath; (3) right beside him, sleeve touching his sleeve, an 11-year-old girl in a white lace dress and white gloves, chin raised, bored and haughty; (4) a 7-year-old girl in a headscarf and long coat, standing on a low stool, clutching a crumpled ribbon in both fists at chest height, looking straight into the lens. Soft north window light from the upper left, gentle shadows to the right, eye-level view from a large wooden camera on a tripod. Warm sepia albumen toning, fine grain, slight edge vignette, a mood of fragile solemn joy. Only the photograph itself: no card mount, no border, no text.

### ch01_02_atelier — 16:9 — обязательный
*Ателье утром. Камера, чёрное покрывало, рука старика поднимает латунную птичку.*
> Interior of a small provincial photo atelier in Kiev, early morning, 1905. In the foreground from behind: a large wooden view camera on a heavy tripod, its black focusing cloth hanging over the back; a man's hand (aged about 45, ink-stained fingers, sleeve rolled up) raises a small brass bird-shaped whistle above the camera. In the soft background: a painted canvas backdrop with a balustrade, a worn carpet, a bentwood chair, a cast-iron stove with a glowing door, shelves of glass plates in wooden boxes. Tall north window, pale October light, window glass fogged with condensation. Dust in the light beam. Quiet, warm, a working place loved for twenty years. Warm sepia.

### ch01_04_square — 16:9 — обязательный

> A vast jubilant crowd on Kreshchatyk in front of the City Duma building, Kiev, midday, 18 October 1905. Strangers embrace, men throw caps in the air, a student clings to a lamppost waving his hat, flags above the heads (appearing dark grey in black and white), a woman laughing with tears on her face in the foreground, a worker with a small girl on his shoulders. The Duma building's balcony in the background, crowded with people. Motion blur on moving hands and caps, a slightly tilted handheld-feeling frame from within the crowd, crisp cold autumn sun, long shadows. Euphoria on the edge of chaos. Warm sepia.

### ch01_06_gateway — 16:9 — желательный
*Подворотня: мальчишка зажимает рассечённую бровь.*
> A dark archway passage of a Kiev courtyard, 1905. A thin 12-year-old boy with dark curly hair crouches behind a wooden rubbish bin, pressing his palm over his brow, blood running between his fingers down his wrist; another boy's hands grip his sleeve from the edge of the frame. Beyond the arch, the bright street is overexposed, with blurred running figures. Deep shadow inside the arch, one shaft of light on the wounded boy's face and hand. Fear, breath held. Warm sepia.

### ch01_07_feathers — 16:9 — обязательный
*Ночь на Подоле. Пух из распоротых перин падает, как снег.*
> Night street in the Podil district of Kiev, October 1905, seen through a courtyard arch. Down and feathers from ripped featherbeds fill the air and fall slowly like snow, lit by several torches carried by dark figures in caps; a featherbed lies slit open on the cobbles; broken windows on the second floor, a curtain hanging out. The figures are silhouettes, faces not visible. Torchlight is the only light source: flickering highlights on the feathers, deep black shadows. Silent, dreamlike horror. Warm sepia darkened almost to black.

### ch01_08_darkroom — 16:9 — обязательный (красный свет — единственный цвет игры)
*Проявочная: полки со стеклом, старик и два мальчика, щель жёлтого света под дверью.*
> A tiny photographic darkroom, night, 1905. Floor-to-ceiling wooden shelves packed with glass plate negatives standing on edge. A dim red safelight lantern turned down to an ember. An older bearded man about 45 and two 12-year-old boys press themselves against the shelves, faces barely modelled by the red glow, one boy with a bandaged brow; the boys hold hands. At the bottom of the closed door, a thin sharp strip of yellow torchlight. Developing trays on a bench, a wet gleam. Deep red monochrome image, everything else black. Suffocating stillness.

### ch01_09_shards — 16:9 — обязательный
*Утро после погрома: пол из битых стеклянных негативов, в пальцах старика осколок с лицом.*
> Morning after the pogrom inside a wrecked photo atelier, Kiev, 1905. The floor is covered in a thick layer of broken glass plate negatives; tiny ghostly faces of strangers — brides, infants, gymnasium students — glimmer in the shards. In the foreground, the ink-stained fingers of a man about 45 hold one shard up to the grey window light: on it, half of a stranger's face, one eye and an ear, reversed like a negative. In the background, the smashed shop window with sparrows on the sill and an untouched painted balustrade backdrop. Soft grey dawn light, total quiet. Warm sepia.
